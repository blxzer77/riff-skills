# Tallyboard product handbook

Tallyboard is a project and billing tool for small agencies. This handbook is the support team's reference.

## 16. Refunds

16.1. Annual plans are refundable in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, refunds are prorated by unused full months.

16.2. Monthly plans are not refundable; cancellation stops the next renewal.

16.3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 business days.
